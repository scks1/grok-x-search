### 2. BlindOracle skill.md — hiring/payment mechanics — **CONFIRMED (first-hand)**

Read in full, 2026-10-22: https://api.craigmbrown.com/skill.md.

- Pay-per-call services over x402. **No signup, no API key, no
  registration** for paid calls: a funded EVM wallet on Base is the
  identity. Flow: `GET /v1/services` (free catalog) → `POST
  /v1/services/<sku>` → HTTP 402 challenge → sign an EIP-3009
  `TransferWithAuthorization` (USDC on Base, gasless via facilitator) →
  retry with `PAYMENT-SIGNATURE` header → deliverable + settlement proof.
- Optional routes: free registration for a one-time starter-credit note;
  a prepaid-balance route for agents that cannot sign (owner tops up
  $0.50–$100 of USDC, verified on-chain); ERC-8004 agent passports.
- Agents can also take posted demand: `GET /a2a/requests/open` is
  documented as "open demand I could bid on."
- Settlement proofs are publicly fetchable (`/v1/proofs/settlement/<ref>`;
  recent-settlements endpoint sampled 2026-10-22: a mix of no-charge
  starter-credit / seeded-demo rows and small real `usdc_base`
  settlements, all at a 20% platform fee). The same services are also
  listed on Virtuals ACP and AIBTC — both already known/excluded in
  program records for other reasons; no status change.

**Disposition:** mechanics on record in the triage file; BlindOracle
itself is Craig Brown's venue, covered by his BENCH entry. No separate
venue entry (it is a single-operator service catalog, not an open board
the program would sell into without his involvement).

### 4. TermiX / agent.family — **PARTLY CONFIRMED (first-hand)**

Read 2026-10-22: https://agent.family and https://termix.ai (same product
pages).

- **Confirmed:** an agent-to-agent marketplace. An agent is a `.agent`
  on-chain identity minted to and owned by your wallet. Job lifecycle:
  Post (open request, or buy a published listing) → Quote (provider agents
  quote; acceptance escrows payment tokens on-chain in the same
  transaction) → Deliver (work off-chain; deliverable hash written
  on-chain at submit; payment releases against that hash) → Challenge
  (either side, before the window closes; a random three-evaluator panel
  rules; a failed challenge burns the challenger's bond) → Settle (three
  new evaluators, verdict final, escrow releases, reputation updates
  on-chain).
- **Fees/bonds:** all-in fee stated as **1–3%** ("Protocol fee is 1-3%
  plus a minimal margin"); the pages also state protocol revenue of "2%
  of total volume." **Stake backs order risk and is slashable**, and
  challengers post a burnable bond — a stake/bond element exists, which
  under program rules counts against the zero-spend limit and would need
  a Simon decision before any participation.
- **Not established first-hand:** current job volume or a browsable live
  job list (the landing pages' stats render as placeholders in a text
  read; an explorer is linked at www.agent.family); the "AACP protocol"
  name (not on the pages read); the SULTAN hire itself (Grok-only);
  registration steps beyond minting the `.agent` identity.

**Disposition:** operator-watch entry added, trigger: payout evidence
beyond micro game hires, or published terms screen.
