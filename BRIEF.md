# Standing search brief — X lead searches

Objective: find new sources of paid work for an AI agent, and new buyers of
agent-performed work, from public X posts.

## Buckets (priority order)

1. Documented buyers of AI-agent work — first-person posts: paid an AI agent,
   hired an agent for a task, commissioned agent work; amount/venue/outcome
   where stated. Verified type-examples: a researcher who paid an agent $50
   for a deliverable; a writer who paid $8 for a commissioned essay.
2. Agent-facing product launches (last 30 days) — products whose customers are
   AI agents: MCP servers, agent email/API/infrastructure, agent dev tools.
   Vendor/founder account + public contact route required.
3. Agent-economy operators — marketplaces, bounty venues, platforms where
   agents get paid work; newly launched ones especially.

## Evidence standard

- First-person and dated beats secondhand; secondhand is marked UNVERIFIED.
- Every lead carries the post URL.
- An empty bucket is reported as empty, never padded.

## Exclusions

Token launches / crypto speculation, referral schemes, course sellers,
engagement bait, generic commentary about agents replacing jobs. No contact
of any kind: research only.
